Teaching & workshops

Since 2018 I have taught and led art workshops in museums, community spaces and camps across the United States and China, in English and Chinese: from stop-motion animation for children to storyboarding and self-reflective drawing for adults.

Walker Art Center, Minneapolis — Free First Saturday stop-motion workshops for children.
Weisman Art Museum — co-creation for The World Inside You, a mental-health art project with the Creativity Camp Study.
MCAD — Jerome Fellowship resident and exhibition.
IOMA Art Center, Beijing — animation script and storyboard workshops, 2020–2021.
Doujia Art Camp, Dali — summer drawing camps for young people, 2025 and 2026.

Screenings and talks have also taken me to SooVAC, abC Art Book Fair, Houshan Art Space, Yushe Space and Rochester Art Center.
