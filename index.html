Youyang Yu 俞悠洋

Making inner worlds visible.

I am an artist, educator and community organiser. Through moving images, drawing, installation and participatory practice, I explore time, the body and belonging.

Currently · Wilder Mountain Dojo

A place to rest. A world to grow.

I am developing Wilder Mountain Dojo / 野山道场, a participatory public art project and a “life cultivation game” that invites people to see time as a flowing landscape, and to cultivate and play within. It makes room for rest, expression and mutual support for people practising self-support through partings, reunions and upheaval.

Basecamp · Twin Cities · November 2026
Three two-hour adult workshops are planned for three Saturday afternoons, supported by the Watson Foundation Enlight Fellowship.
